Howdy, and welcome

Who we are

The Aggie Dog Walking Society is a student org for anyone at A&M who likes dogs, owns one or not. We started in 2019 when a handful of Corps and off-campus students kept running into each other on the same trail every Sunday morning, and decided it would be more fun to walk together.

Today we're about 140 members: undergrads, grad students, a few very patient roommates, and somewhere north of sixty dogs. We keep things low-key: show up when you can, bring your pup if you have one, and if you don't, the shelter dogs are always glad to see you.

Three ways to show up

What we do

Most of what we do happens outside, on a leash, at a pace a dachshund can keep up with.

Weekly group walks

Every Sunday morning

A relaxed two-mile loop along the Wolf Pen Creek Trail. Walk leaders split the group into a chill pace and a brisk pace, and we finish back at the amphitheater for water bowls and belly rubs.

Shelter dog walks

First and third Saturdays

We carpool to the Brazos Paws Shelter (a fictional partner) and take adoptable dogs out for their best hour of the week. No dog of your own needed; shelter staff pair you with a dog that fits your experience.

Member potlucks

Last Friday of the month

Dinner under the pavilion at Bee Creek Park. Bring a dish to share, or a bag of chips if it's finals week. Well-behaved dogs welcome; there's a separate snack table for them.

Your first walk

You don't need to sign up in advance or know anybody. Here's exactly what a first Sunday looks like.

  1. 8:50 a.m. — Find the maroon flag. A walk leader with a maroon ADWS flag waits by the amphitheater stage. Tell them it's your first time and they'll introduce you around.
  2. 9:00 a.m. — Quick dog check-in. If you brought a dog, the leader asks its name and how it does with other dogs, then gives you a colored bandana: green for social, yellow for "needs space."
  3. 9:10 a.m. — Pick a pace. Chill group or brisk group, about two miles either way. No dog? Walk with whoever you like, and people will happily share leash time.
  4. 10:00 a.m. — Cool down. Water bowls, treats, and a lot of dog photos. Plenty of folks head to breakfast together afterwards.

What to bring and wear:

  • Comfortable shoes
  • A water bottle
  • A 6-ft leash (no retractables)
  • Poop bags
  • Sunscreen and a hat
  • Proof of rabies vaccine for your dog

Getting there

Find us

Sunday walks start at the Wolf Pen Creek Park amphitheater, off Dartmouth Street near Post Oak Mall in College Station.

Driving
Free parking in the lot next to the amphitheater. It fills by 9 on football weekends, so overflow parking is along Dartmouth.
Bus
Off-campus bus routes stop on Holleman Drive, about a five-minute walk to the park entrance.
Biking
Bike racks are by the amphitheater restrooms. The creek trail connects to the Holleman bike lanes.
Can't find us?
Look for the maroon flag and the cluster of very happy dogs on the lawn below the stage.

Before you ask

Common questions

The handful of things almost every new member wants to know. Still curious? Ask any walk leader on Sunday; they've heard every question at least twice.

Do I need a dog to join?

Not at all. About a third of our members don't have a dog. Come to the shelter walks, borrow leash time from a friend on Sundays, or just come for the potlucks. You're a full member either way.

Does it cost anything?

No dues. Potlucks are bring-a-dish, and the shelter carpool is free. We occasionally sell club bandanas and T-shirts, but nobody is ever required to buy one.

My dog is reactive or shy. Can we still come?

Yes. Tell us in the signup form and on the day. Yellow-bandana dogs walk at the back of the group with extra space, and a walk leader checks in with you before we start. If your dog needs a quieter start, the Wednesday stroll is smaller.

What vaccines does my dog need?

Current rabies and DHPP, the same as most Texas dog parks. Keep a photo of the rabies certificate on your phone; walk leaders may ask the first time you come.

Can I bring a puppy?

Puppies are welcome once they've finished their full vaccine series, usually around four months. Shorter legs are fine: the chill-pace group is happy to stop for every interesting smell.

What happens if the weather is bad?

We walk in light rain. Lightning, ice, or a heat index over 100°F cancels the walk, and we post that in the group chat by 7 a.m. on the day.

I'm a grad student. Am I welcome?

Absolutely. Any current A&M student can join: undergrads, grad students, and professional students alike.
